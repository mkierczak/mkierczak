RaukR

Since 2018, together with colleagues, I organise RaukR, the Advanced R Programming for Bioinformatics summer school held on the island of Gotland in Visby, Sweden. The course covers writing robust, efficient and reproducible R for life-science data. Course materials and information about upcoming editions are at raukr.nbis.se.

Talks and popular science

From mythology to artificial intelligence: a short journey through statistical models (in Swedish: Från mytologi till artificiell intelligens. En kort resa genom statistiska modeller)
Public lecture in the 13x13 series, Uppsala University, 5 November 2025, Humanistiska Teatern. The talk follows how people have tried to understand the world, from myth to modern statistical models and AI: how such models are built, how reliable they are, and what to look out for when a new AI model appears. 13x13 is a series of thirteen free lectures, organised by the Department of Physics and Astronomy, in which Uppsala researchers present their work to a general audience.
