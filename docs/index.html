Marcin Kierczak, PhD

Training & Industry Outreach Coordinator · Data Scientist · NBIS

I coordinate training and industry outreach at the National Bioinformatics Infrastructure Sweden (NBIS), part of SciLifeLab, connecting Swedish life science with the bioinformatics skills and expertise it needs. As a data scientist at NBIS, I also work with research groups on their data analysis. In addition, I am an associate professor of bioinformatics at Uppsala University, with a research background in machine learning, statistical genomics and ancient DNA.

What I do

Training

Industry outreach

Ancient & conservation genomics

Genome erosion, demographic history and adaptation in endangered and extinct species.

Metagenomics

Metabarcoding and community profiling, from food quality control to ecology.
